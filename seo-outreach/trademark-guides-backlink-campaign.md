## Qualified outreach tracks

### Branding and naming publications

- Pitch: “A business-name search is not a trademark search: a practical checklist for founders.”
- Useful destination: the trademark search guide or registration checklist.
- Example public contribution opportunities identified during research:
  - `https://www.brandingx.net/write-for-us/`
  - `https://www.namestall.com/blog/submit-guest-post/`
- Requirement: disclose the commercial relationship and provide original, non-promotional material.

### Startup and small-business organizations

- Pitch: offer the application checklist as a free resource for founder resource libraries.
- Targets: local chambers of commerce, entrepreneurship centers, incubators, and SCORE-adjacent educational partners.
- Destination: the application checklist, not the sales landing page.

### E-commerce and creator publications

- Pitch: “Trademark preparation checklist for marketplace sellers and direct-to-consumer brands.”
- Targets: e-commerce agencies, seller newsletters, creator-business publications, and marketplace consultants.
- Destination: the registration-process or classes guide.

### Branding agencies and web studios

- Pitch: add the plain-language trademark definition or naming checklist to client launch resources.
- Offer: a co-branded checklist or expert contribution that links to the underlying guide.
- Avoid reciprocal sitewide footer links.

## Quality controls

- Earn links to educational resources, not keyword-stuffed links to the sales page.
- Prefer branded and natural anchors such as “Legal Trademark Office trademark cost guide.”
- Do not buy links, use private blog networks, automate mass submissions, or request exact-match anchors.
- Verify that every placement is editorially relevant and publicly accessible.
- Track outreach date, response, published URL, anchor text, and target guide.

## Initial KPIs

- 10 carefully personalized pitches per week.
- 2 guest-contribution pitches per month.
- 2–5 new relevant referring domains in the first 90 days.
- No exact-match anchor concentration above a natural minority of the profile.
