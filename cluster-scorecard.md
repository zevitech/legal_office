## Quality controls

- All content is server rendered and visible without client-side fetching.
- The Organization is the publisher; no person, credential, review, rating, or attorney identity was invented.
- Google-restricted commercial `FAQPage` markup is not used.
- Fees and time-sensitive procedures point readers to current USPTO sources.
- The pages use distinct intents and do not create state or city duplicates for a federal service.

## External limitations

The scorecard measures the implemented website architecture. It does not claim rankings, keyword volume, external backlinks, human editorial credentials, or third-party reputation. Those require independent data and earned evidence after deployment.
