# Brand Reputation Recovery — Legal Trademark Office

Date reviewed: August 24, 2026

## Confirmed search risk

Google can find negative third-party discussions about the business. Public search results also show a Trustpilot profile with a 3.3/5 score from six reviews and a BBB profile with an F rating attributed to two unanswered complaints. The website and Google's older cached copy contain attorney, guarantee, review-volume, and customer-count claims that require substantiation and are inconsistent with the independent filing-support positioning.

This cannot be fixed with schema, backlinks, or keyword changes alone. Search and AI systems compare first-party claims with independent sources.

## Immediate operational actions

1. Identify and investigate every BBB, Trustpilot, Google, and Reddit complaint against transaction records.
2. Respond to both BBB complaints through the official business portal. Resolve valid billing or filing problems and document the outcome.
3. Respond individually to Trustpilot reviews without exposing customer information. Offer a real escalation contact and a case review.
4. If a review demonstrably violates a platform policy, report it with supporting evidence. Do not report a review merely because it is negative.
5. Stop any unexpected post-purchase charges. Obtain explicit written approval for every optional service and show the amount and purpose before charging.
6. Make invoices distinguish the private service fee, USPTO government fee, class count, and any optional later-stage service.

## Website corrections required

- Remove attorney names, attorney imagery, legal-representation claims, and office-action legal-response claims unless each is supported by a real U.S.-licensed attorney relationship that can be publicly verified.
- Remove or qualify registration guarantees, “Trademark Insurance,” success claims, customer totals, ratings, accreditations, and media logos unless documentary evidence and exact terms are available.
- Use “USPTO filing fee,” not “Federal Office” or language that resembles a government agency.
- Present the complete expected checkout amount and optional-service rules before payment.
- Add a visible customer-care and complaint-resolution process with a monitored email, response target, billing-dispute steps, and correction policy.
- Keep the independent-service disclosure near pricing and checkout, not only in the footer.

## Search and AI recovery actions

1. Submit factual feedback on an inaccurate AI Overview using its thumbs-down and “Report a problem” controls. Include evidence; do not claim that genuine customer reports are false without proof.
2. After website corrections deploy, request recrawling of the homepage and landing page in Search Console.
3. Publish a factual service-scope and fee-transparency page only after the operating process matches it.
4. Earn legitimate branded mentions through resolved customer experiences, verified profiles, and useful USPTO-sourced educational resources.
5. Request honest reviews from all eligible customers without incentives or review gating.

## Prohibited shortcuts

Do not purchase positive reviews, incentivize review removal, impersonate customers, mass-report criticism, threaten reviewers, create fake Reddit discussions, buy reputation backlinks, or publish unverifiable rebuttals. Those actions can deepen the trust problem and violate platform policies.

## Measurement

Review the branded search result weekly, record cited AI Overview sources, monitor BBB and Trustpilot response status, track the ratio of resolved complaints, and measure branded-search sentiment. Expect recovery to depend on recrawling and new independent evidence; no date or outcome can be guaranteed.
