# Backlink and Brand Authority Plan

Target market: United States  
Primary ranking URL: `https://www.legaltrademarkoffice.com/trademark-registration`  
Primary commercial keyword: `trademark registration`

## Data status

Only Common Crawl and local link verification are available. Moz and Bing Webmaster backlink APIs are not connected, and the local Common Crawl script cannot run until its `requests` dependency is available. No numeric backlink score or referring-domain count should be claimed from this data.

## Safe acquisition priorities

1. Seek branded homepage citations from legitimate U.S. business directories and profiles where the company is eligible and business information can be verified.
2. Pitch the registration-process and cost guides to U.S. small-business resource libraries, incubators, entrepreneurship centers, chambers, university entrepreneurship programs, and relevant trade associations.
3. Offer the search guide as a neutral educational reference for brand-development, naming, e-commerce, and startup resources.
4. Build earned mentions through useful commentary, original data, and genuinely helpful resources—not purchased placements.
5. Reclaim real unlinked brand mentions only after verifying the page and publisher.

## Anchor policy

- Prefer brand anchors: `Legal Trademark Office` and the domain name.
- Use descriptive educational anchors for guide links.
- Keep exact-match `trademark registration` external anchors uncommon and editorially natural.
- Never use automated directory blasts, private blog networks, sitewide paid links, or mass guest-post packages.

## Link-worthy assets to build next

- A current USPTO fee reference with dated source checks.
- A printable pre-filing checklist.
- A plain-language federal application timeline.
- A trademark-class finder or structured class-selection worksheet, with clear limitations.
- Original, anonymized filing-process data only if the company can document the source and methodology.

## Measurement

Track referring domains, branded searches, guide impressions, and assisted conversions monthly. Once Bing Webmaster or Moz is connected, establish a sourced baseline before evaluating growth or toxicity. Backlinks must be evaluated for relevance and editorial legitimacy, not raw quantity.
