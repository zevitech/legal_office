# Trademark Content and Authority Roadmap

## Publishing standard

Every educational page must:

- answer the main question before promoting a service;
- cite current primary sources from the USPTO or another responsible government agency beside material claims;
- distinguish Legal Trademark Office filing support from USPTO examination and approval;
- show published and reviewed dates;
- use one H1, descriptive H2/H3 sections, a self-canonical, Article schema and BreadcrumbList schema;
- link to the main guide and at least two closely related guides;
- avoid invented authors, credentials, reviews, success rates or guarantees;
- state when information is general education rather than advice for a reader's individual situation.

Fees, deadlines, forms and processing-time statements require quarterly review. Stable fundamentals require annual review.

The commercial head term remains owned only by `/trademark-registration`. Do not create separate top-level `/trademark-application`, `/trademark-cost`, or `/trademark-attorney` pages unless Search Console and SERP-overlap evidence later proves that a genuinely different commercial page is needed. Their current informational intent is served inside `/guides`, preventing avoidable cannibalization and unsupported service positioning.

## Trust evidence required from the business

Before adding stronger E-E-A-T claims, collect and verify:

- the legal business name and genuine public business address;
- the names and real roles of people responsible for educational content;
- any reviewer credentials, with license jurisdiction and public verification link when applicable;
- a factual description of the internal content-review and corrections process;
- sources and date ranges for customer counts, ratings, media logos and performance claims;
- written terms for any satisfaction, refund or insurance statement;
- anonymized first-party examples that customers have authorized for publication.

Until evidence exists, use the Organization entity as publisher and do not add Person, Attorney, LegalService, Review or AggregateRating markup.
