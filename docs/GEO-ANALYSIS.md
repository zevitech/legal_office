## Platform outlook

- **Google AI Overviews:** technically ready after deployment and indexing; ranking and reputation remain limiting factors.
- **ChatGPT search:** server-rendered resources, organization schema, and `/llms.txt` improve discoverability; independent brand mentions remain necessary.
- **Perplexity:** quotable source-backed passages are available; reputation and community-source balance remain weak.
- **Bing Copilot:** sitemap-ready; Bing Webmaster connection and IndexNow remain optional next integrations.

## Implemented GEO signals

- Twenty distinct resources organized into four topic clusters.
- Answer-first summaries that can stand alone when extracted.
- Current USPTO citations beside every guide.
- Article, CollectionPage, ItemList, and BreadcrumbList schema.
- Visible publisher and review dates without invented person credentials.
- Editorial standards and corrections route.
- `/llms.txt` index of high-value educational pages.
- Server-rendered HTML with no client fetch required for guide content.

## Remaining authority work

1. Earn legitimate branded mentions and educational citations from relevant U.S. organizations.
2. Resolve real customer complaints and seek updated reviews through neutral, non-incentivized processes.
3. Add named human authors or reviewers only after their identity, role, permission, and credentials are verifiable.
4. Publish original anonymized process data only when methodology and customer authorization can be documented.
5. Add useful original media such as a printable checklist or timeline graphic.

AI platforms choose their own sources. These changes improve eligibility and citation clarity; they cannot guarantee inclusion or control how third-party discussions are summarized.
